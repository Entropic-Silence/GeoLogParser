# Data rights and attribution

This archive has no blanket licence. Source-specific terms and attribution
continue to apply. The sole project owner manually reviewed and approved the
selected `data-v002` item scope for public research distribution on 2026-08-20.
This record is not a legal opinion and does not alter upstream rights.

| Source family | Recorded terms or release basis | Required attribution and caveats |
|---|---|---|
| California USGS lithology transcription | CC0 1.0 / public domain as stated by the official release | Retain USGS data-release citation, DOI, record linkage, and modification provenance. |
| California public WCR reports | Public redacted report links paired to the CC0 table; selected report and embedded-content scope manually reviewed for this release | Retain USGS and California Department of Water Resources source linkage; do not infer that the CC0 table statement grants a blanket licence to unrelated embedded material. |
| BGS offshore and BGS v001 | OGL v3 is stated in the official BGS records | Retain British Geological Survey acknowledgement, source URLs, OGL reference, and the embedded legacy-footer caveat. |
| Swissgeol Thurgau v003 | No standardized licence is asserted by this project; selected public-profile and database item scope manually reviewed for `data-v002` | Retain swisstopo/swissgeol attribution, public record/profile URLs, and linkage metadata. Downstream users must assess their own reuse scope. |
| USGS Raft River | USGS public-domain data release; included embedded IDWR form scope manually reviewed | Retain the USGS DOI/citation and Idaho Department of Water Resources attribution where present. |
| Mendeley Coal 602 | CC BY 4.0 | Retain creator, title, DOI `10.17632/33z3d5r6xk.1`, licence, and modification notice. Coordinates remain linkable and are not represented as anonymous. |
