# Supplementary Material for Paper I

## S1. Scope

This supplement retains acquisition, eligibility, degradation-inventory, CAD-conversion, Machine-Silver, no-reference coverage, and single-source diagnostic material that is useful for reproducibility but cannot support the same claims as Published manual transcription Gold. The main manuscript limits performance inference to explicitly tiered California, Swissgeol, BGS, Raft River, and source-shift results.

## S2. Candidate-source and annotation-system audits

- Padova, slopes/Tiber, SedLog, and Chinese CAD candidates retain licence, retrieval, renderer, source-review, and annotation-state manifests.
- Padova duplicate-track infrastructure contains no effective human attestation and supplies no project-created Gold result.
- Chinese CAD conversion and four-panel VLM runs remain rights-unverified or conversion-incomplete and are excluded from accuracy tables.

## S3. Secondary diagnostics

- USGS-142 and USGS-144 are tailored single-document transfer checks.
- USGS Idaho cross-engine counts are detection-agreement events, not accuracy.
- BGS degradation transforms evaluate identifier/coordinate fields only.
- Padova Machine-Silver comparisons report agreement to a machine-constructed reference.
- Slopes, Tiber, SedLog, Padova, and quarantined Chinese runs report coverage, schema validity, candidate counts, latency, or failure type only.

All exact experiment IDs, evidence tiers, hashes, and numeric outputs remain in [current results](generated/current_results.md), the claim registry, and the publication-evidence bundle.
